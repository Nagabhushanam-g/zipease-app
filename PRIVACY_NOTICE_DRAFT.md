# ZipEase privacy notice — draft, NOT for publication without review

ZipEase lets a user choose files locally and compress/extract ZIP archives on the device. Archive contents are not deliberately sent to ZipEase servers. Input files and temporary results may be cached in private app storage and are subject to device storage/cache behaviour.

The testing build integrates Google Mobile Ads SDK with **Google test ad units**; the SDK may collect device information or other data under Google's policies even when serving test ads. A production version must use a reviewed privacy policy, accurate Play Console Data Safety disclosures, and consent requirements appropriate to served regions. Links to Google advertising/privacy policies and a user contact address must be added before publication.

This is an engineering draft only, not a verified store-ready legal notice.
